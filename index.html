Our Story

Born from a Passion
for Perfection

Founded in 2008, LUMIÈRE was born from a single belief: that fine jewelry should be more than adornment — it should be a testament to the moments that define you.

Every piece we create begins with a story. Our master jewelers work with the finest ethically-sourced materials, from conflict-free diamonds to 18-karat gold, transforming raw beauty into wearable art.

From our atelier to your hands — each creation carries the weight of generations of craft and the lightness of pure joy.
